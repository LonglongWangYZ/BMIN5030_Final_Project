# BMIN5030_Final_Project
BMIN5030 Final Project Yizhou Wang

1. Overview
   
This project investigates whether a small panel of cardiovascular-related genes can distinguish patients with coronary artery disease (CAD) from healthy controls using publicly available gene-expression data. Using the GSE42148 dataset, I aim to identify potentially informative genes and evaluate their predictive performance through exploratory data analysis and statistical modeling in R.

2. Introduction
   
Coronary artery disease (CAD) is a leading cause of cardiovascular morbidity and mortality worldwide. Although genetic and environmental factors contribute to CAD development, identifying molecular signatures associated with the disease remains challenging. Gene-expression profiling provides an opportunity to investigate differences in biological activity between CAD patients and healthy individuals. This project uses the publicly available GSE42148 whole-blood gene-expression dataset to explore whether a small set of cardiovascular-related genes can distinguish the two groups.

This project combines cardiovascular biology, genetics, bioinformatics, and statistical modeling. Cardiovascular biology provides insight into disease mechanisms, while bioinformatics and data science enable the analysis of gene-expression patterns and evaluation of predictive models. My initial plan involved integrating CAD GWAS findings with GTEx expression data, but preliminary research led me to focus on a smaller, more manageable gene-expression dataset. I plan to incorporate feedback from faculty and staff to refine gene selection, analytical methods, and interpretation of the results.
